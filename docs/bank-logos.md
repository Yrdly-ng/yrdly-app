Bank logos are bundled locally so saved accounts and bank pickers do not depend on a runtime mapping request. The catalogue matches exact normalized bank names and reviewed aliases before codes because Payluk NIP codes and legacy codes differ. Never use fuzzy matching or assign a logo based on a truncated code. Unknown banks and image failures display initials.

Source: the existing YRDLY bank-logos storage library, originally imported from https://github.com/supermx1/nigerian-banks-api. Generic placeholder images are excluded. The catalogue contains 124 bank image assets; this is not full coverage of every institution in the provider list. Bank names, code aliases and original image filenames are versioned in src/lib/payments/bank-logo-catalogue.json. Add original brand artwork plus exact names/aliases there in both applications; mobile also requires a static entry in src/lib/payments/bank-logo-assets.ts. Keep the two catalogues and images synchronized. Do not guess a brand for an unlisted institution.
