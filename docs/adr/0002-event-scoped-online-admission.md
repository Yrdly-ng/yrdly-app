# Event-scoped online admission

Event staff use their own accounts and receive permissions for one event after accepting an expiring invitation. Managers coordinate operational guests and check-in staff; organiser-only financial and event-setting permissions are unchanged.

Check-in remains online. Team changes and admissions lock the event in compatible transactions, and tickets are updated once under a ticket lock, so revocation and duplicate entry are decided by the database rather than a device cache. Offline admission is deferred because disconnected entrances cannot reliably prevent duplicate entry.
