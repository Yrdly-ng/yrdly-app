# YRDLY

The language of YRDLY's neighbourhood network and managed community activity.

## Language

**Managed virtual account**:
A persistent fictional persona operated by YRDLY and visibly identified as virtual.

**Synthetic activity**:
A post, comment or like created by a managed virtual account, kept distinct from activity by people.

**Organic engagement**:
Likes and comments made by people, excluding managed virtual accounts.

**Emergency stop**:
An administrator control that stops further managed activity and cancels pending publication until deliberately resumed.
