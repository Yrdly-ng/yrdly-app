# YRDLY

The language of YRDLY events and their admission teams.

## Language

### Event admission

**Event team**:
The people an organiser authorises to help admit guests or coordinate attendance for one event.

**Event manager**:
An accepted event team member who can coordinate guests and check-in staff, without control over event pricing, payments, refunds or organiser ownership.

**Check-in staff**:
An accepted event team member authorised to validate tickets and admit guests for one event.

**Event team invitation**:
An account-bound offer of an event role that expires after seven days unless accepted. An unaccepted invitation grants no admission permissions.

**Check-in history**:
The record of successful ticket admissions, including the time and the person who admitted each guest.
